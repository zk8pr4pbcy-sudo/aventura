Al-Balad · A living story

Enter the city one threshold at a time

Before the walk begins, your guide reads your interests and adjusts the pace, turning each alley into a personal way into Historic Jeddah.

We do not rush the story. We leave room to notice the Roshan, stone, sounds and small details.

  1. 01

    Meet your guide

    Your guide is more than a voice telling stories; they are a living memory that knows every doorway by name and every alley by its story.

  2. 02

    Read the Roshan

    The Roshan is not understood from the street alone. Its shadows shift with the hours, and its carving holds a story left untold.

  3. 03

    القهوة العربية والتمر ومحطات تراثية مختارة

    The walk ends where time—not the schedule—decides: a cup of Arabic coffee, a date, and enough quiet to hear the city.
