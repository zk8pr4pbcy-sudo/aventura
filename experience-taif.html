Taif highlands · A quieter rhythm

Trade the city heat for mountain air and a gentler day

A private Taif journey brings together cooler air, mountain contours, roses and seasonal local detail at a pace that lets the highlands breathe.

The season shapes the detail; the calm of the highlands shapes everything else.

  1. 01

    Climb into a different rhythm

  2. 02

    Meet the season in Taif

    Roses, orchards and local stops are chosen around what the highlands genuinely offer at that time of year.

  3. 03

    Pause where the view opens

    The day leaves space for the landscape, the air and the unplanned moments that make Taif feel unlike Jeddah.
