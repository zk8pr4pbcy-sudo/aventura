# Aventura KSA — Social Launch Light Identity

## Approved account direction

- Primary username: `@aventuraksa`
- Display name: `Aventura KSA | أفنتورا`
- Website: `https://aventuraksa.com`
- Content priority: Historic Jeddah first, then the Red Sea, desert, and Taif.

## Visual direction

Aventura social content must feel bright, warm, active, realistic, and distinctly Saudi. Dark or gloomy brand systems are not approved for launch.

### Approved palette

- Warm ivory: `#FFF9EF`
- Light sand: `#F2E4CC`
- Soft Red Sea blue: `#DCEFF2`
- Taif green: `#DCE8D6`
- Taif rose: `#EBCFD2`
- Warm gold: `#C59A55`
- Text navy only: `#17324A`

### Prohibited treatment

- Black backgrounds
- Full-page dark navy backgrounds
- Heavy black overlays on destination photos
- Artificial-looking AI tourism imagery
- Empty luxury visuals with no Saudi sense of place
- Making sea and desert both look like the same sunset campaign

## Image policy

- Use Aventura-owned photography and approved real stock photography.
- Preserve natural daylight and true local colors.
- Show real movement, hospitality, interaction, streets, architecture, sea activity, dunes, and Taif landscapes.
- Historic Jeddah leads the launch grid and brand story.

## Platform execution

### Instagram

- Feed ratio: 4:5 when possible.
- First nine posts must form a bright, balanced grid.
- Reels use full-screen 9:16 video, captions, natural sound, and real human moments.

### TikTok

- Native 9:16 video.
- Real people, movement, behind-the-scenes planning, and short experience stories.
- Avoid polished corporate-ad styling that feels detached from the platform.

### Snapchat

- Fast, immediate, location-led coverage.
- Use daylight, arrival moments, preparation, and live experience details.

### LinkedIn

- Bright professional photography.
- Focus on corporate programs, executive guests, event operations, partnerships, and Saudi hospitality.

## Launch message

**Arabic:** نصنع تجارب تُحكى

**English:** Saudi experiences worth sharing

## Brand description

أفنتورا علامة سعودية متخصصة في تصميم وتنفيذ التجارب الخاصة والعامة، وتنظيم الفعاليات، وخدمات الشركات والضيوف التنفيذيين وVIP، مع خدمات الضيافة والكونسيرج.

Aventura KSA curates and operates private and public Saudi experiences, events, corporate programs, executive and VIP hospitality, supported by integrated concierge and guest services.
