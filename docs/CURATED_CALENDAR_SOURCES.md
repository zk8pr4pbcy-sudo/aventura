# Aventura Curated Calendar — source and linking rules

Last reviewed: 2026-09-10

## Purpose

The Curated Calendar may link visitors to an event organiser's official website or to an independent ticketing platform. Aventura must not present those outbound destinations as Aventura-owned pages, imply an official partnership that does not exist, or resell third-party tickets unless a separate authorised commercial arrangement is in place.

## Publishing rules

1. Use Aventura-owned, licensed, or otherwise commercially permitted imagery. Do not copy event-site or ticket-platform images merely because a public URL exists.
2. Write event summaries and Aventura recommendations in Aventura's own words. Do not reproduce protected marketing copy from the source.
3. Label outbound links according to their actual destination:
   - organiser/event site: “Official event website ↗”
   - ticketing platform: “Tickets via <platform> ↗”
4. Open external destinations in a new tab and mark them as external links.
5. Keep the Aventura service CTA separate from the external event/ticket link.
6. Aventura's disclaimer must state that Aventura is independent and is not the event organiser, official partner, or ticket seller unless explicitly stated otherwise.
7. Salem must verify the source, event date, venue, and source terms again whenever an event is added or materially updated.

## Sources reviewed for the initial pilot

### Saudi Industry Forum 2026
Official site: https://www.ksa-sif.com/jeddah/

The site's published terms state that event content and materials remain protected by their owners and may not be photographed or published for commercial purposes without prior written permission. Aventura therefore links to the official site but does not reuse its protected media or marketing copy.

### JIBEX 2026
Official site: https://jibex-sa.com/secure-your-visit/

The official site identifies its content as all rights reserved and publishes Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy links. Aventura uses only an outbound source link and Aventura-owned contextual imagery, and does not reproduce JIBEX site media.

### Platinumlist event pages
Platform terms: https://jeddah.platinumlist.net/terms-and-conditions/TermsandConditionsCustomers

Platinumlist describes itself as the ticketing platform and states that it sells tickets on behalf of event promoters, who remain responsible for event organisation and management. Aventura therefore labels these destinations as ticket-platform links, does not present itself as the ticket seller, and does not resell Platinumlist tickets.

English:
Aventura is an independent service provider and is not the event organiser, an official partner, or the ticket seller unless explicitly stated otherwise. Event schedules, admission and tickets remain subject to the organiser or ticketing platform.

Spanish:
Aventura es un proveedor de servicios independiente y no es el organizador del evento, un socio oficial ni el vendedor de entradas, salvo que se indique expresamente lo contrario. Los horarios, el acceso y las entradas dependen del organizador o de la plataforma de venta.
